## OB-13B — REFUTED

**Counterexample 1: (1, 6, 7) — squarefree.**

- Pa=∅, Pb={2,3}, Pc={7}; ω*=3, R=2·3·7=42, v_max=1.
- E_n theorem: nd = second_smallest{∞, 2, 7} = 7. Brute-force verified: nd=7.
- OB-13B claim: nd ≤ v_max·R^{1/(ω*-1)} = 1·√42 ≈ 6.48.
- **7 > 6.48. VIOLATED.**

**Counterexample 2: (1, 30, 31) — squarefree.**

- Pa=∅, Pb={2,3,5}, Pc={31}; ω*=4, R=2·3·5·31=930, v_max=1.
- E_n theorem: nd = second_smallest{∞, 2, 31} = 31. Brute-force verified: nd=31.
- OB-13B claim: nd ≤ 1·930^{1/3} ≈ 9.76.
- **31 > 9.76. VIOLATED by factor > 3.**

Both counterexamples verified by brute-force enumeration
(`discovery/m2_directions/ob13_verify.py`; bound parameter 15).

## OB-13A — REFUTED for squarefree triples

OB-13A asks for constants C≥1, A≥0 with nd ≤ C·v_max^A·R^{1/(ω*-1)} universally.
For squarefree triples v_max=1, so v_max^A=1 for all A.

**Unbounded family:** (1, p₁p₂⋯p_k, p) where p = 1+p₁⋯p_k is prime.
- Pa=∅, Pb={p₁,...,p_k}, Pc={p}; ω*=k+1.
- nd = second_smallest{∞, p₁, p} = p (large prime for large p).
- R^{1/(ω*-1)} = R^{1/k} = (p₁⋯p_k · p)^{1/k} ≈ (p₁⋯p_k)^{1/k} · p^{1/k}.
- nd / R^{1/(ω*-1)} = p / [(p₁⋯p_k)^{1/k} · p^{1/k}] = p^{(k-1)/k} / (p₁⋯p_k)^{1/k} → ∞.

For any fixed C, taking p large enough makes nd > C·R^{1/(ω*-1)}. OB-13A has no
solution for squarefree triples.

## Step 3 — What is proved and what is not

**Proved (correct):** The cross-group construction gives
$$
\mathrm{nd}(a,b) \;\leq\; v_{\max} \cdot \mathrm{med}(m_a, m_b, m_c).
$$
This is tight for squarefree triples: (1,6,7) achieves nd = v_max·med = 1·7 = 7.

**Error in Step 3:** The further step
$$
\mathrm{med}(m_a, m_b, m_c)^{\omega^*-1} \leq R
$$
is false in general. For (1,6,7): med=7, med²=49 > R=42. The argument
"ω*-1 primes other than the smallest all exceed the median" fails because
for primes {2,3,7} the non-minimum primes are {3,7} and 3 < med=7.

**Corrected Step 3 conclusion:** nd ≤ v_max·med(m_a,m_b,m_c) [PROVED].
This does NOT reduce to a bound in R alone.
