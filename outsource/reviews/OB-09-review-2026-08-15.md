## Theorem B: gap patched

The proof was correct but had one implicit step. Patch: any prime ℓ dividing gcd(c_p : p∈P)
must already lie in P (since each c_p = R/p is a product of primes from P only). Then
for p = ℓ: c_ℓ = ∏_{q∈P\{ℓ}} q omits ℓ entirely (squarefree R), so ℓ ∤ c_ℓ,
contradicting ℓ | gcd. Hence gcd = 1.

## Flag: Vaaler's theorem required for Corollary C

Minkowski's theorem bounds vector coordinates in *some orthonormal basis* of the
(ω−1)-dimensional span of L, not automatically in the ambient coordinates ψ(p).
Since L sits in a hyperplane of Z^P, getting the bound ‖ψ‖_∞ ≤ det(L)^{1/(ω−1)}
in *ambient coordinates* requires **Vaaler's theorem (1979)**:
every central hyperplane section of [−1,1]^n has (n−1)-volume ≥ 2^{n−1}.
Combined with Minkowski's convex-body theorem applied to {x∈H : max_p|x_p| ≤ t},
this does recover ‖ψ‖_∞ ≤ det(L)^{1/(ω−1)} — so the conclusion is correct —
but the citation must be "Minkowski + Vaaler (1979)" not "Minkowski alone."
